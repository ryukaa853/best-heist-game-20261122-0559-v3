**The Gilded Hour** — a top-down stealth heist game. You are Sable, the thief the Aurelia Museum never sees coming, working through **five wings** — the Marble Atrium, the Portrait Hall, the Sculpture Court, the Vault Gallery, and the Crown Vault — lifting each wing's masterpiece and slipping out before the alarm meter hits lockdown.

Every wing offers three ways through: the guarded main hall, the camera-watched side gallery, or a slow vent crawl no eye watches. Guards come in five kinds — patrol, stationary watcher, elite (long cone), hound (hears you run), and runner (sprints to where you were last seen) — alongside sweeping cameras and timed laser grids. Loot buys tools at the Fence between wings: smoke pellets, decoy chimes, EMP bursts, a glass cutter, soft soles, and lock picks. Getting caught costs a quarter of the wing's loot and one of three busts. A full story arc: five masterpieces, one crown, one dawn getaway.

Original art, names, levels and synthesised sound; no libraries, no network requests, plain static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold SHIFT to sneak, E to grab loot and crawl vents, 1 smoke, 2 decoy (click where to throw it), 3 EMP, P to pause, M to mute.
